How It Works

Transparent by design — no black boxes.

Scoring Formula

ICP Fit = Σ( weight[param] × presence[param] ) — 21 firmographic/financial params
Intent = Σ( signal_confidence × polarity ) — matched intent signals
RawScore = 0.70 × ICP_Fit + 0.30 × Intent
Network Proximity = graph hops to nearest won deal (separate — never blended)

Confidence Bands

BandTriggerDisplay
HIGH 4+ signals, avg conf ≥ 0.80Show RawScore as-is
MEDIUM 2–3 matched signalsShow RawScore with ±10 band
LOW Fewer than 2 signalsCap at 65, show LOW badge

Confidence is a display modifier, never blended into the score.

Responsible AI

  • No individual-level personal data — scores companies, not people.
  • Every signal retains full provenance (signal_id, source, confidence).
  • Scoring is deterministic — identical inputs always produce identical outputs.
  • Human approval required for every ICP weight change. No silent auto-retraining.
  • Rep overrides logged as feedback — never used as training labels.
  • Temporal leakage prevention: score_eligibility_date ≤ deal_created_date enforced at query layer.
